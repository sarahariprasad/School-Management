How to read this page
  • Each card is one therapist and shows only the hours that therapist works.
  • A therapist can have several students in the same hour. Each student has their own status and ⋮ menu.
  • ✓ next to a time means everyone in that hour is done. Use Complete all to finish a whole day in one click.
  • Recurring slots continue every day until the student becomes inactive. Use Add student for a new slot or a one-off make-up.
